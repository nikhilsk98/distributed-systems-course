Part II Observations

Atomic Counter:
The normal counter had a data race and produced an incorrect result. The atomic counter always produced the expected result.

Plain Map:
Concurrent writes to a normal Go map caused a "concurrent map writes" error.

Mutex:
Mean execution time: 10.73 ms.
Mutex safely protects the map by allowing only one goroutine at a time into the critical section.

RWMutex:
Mean execution time: 11.07 ms.
RWMutex was not faster in this test because the workload was mostly writes. Write locks are still exclusive.

sync.Map:
Mean execution time: 6.65 ms.
sync.Map was the fastest in these runs and safely supported concurrent access.

File Access:
Buffered writing should be faster because many small writes are collected in memory and written in larger batches instead of repeatedly writing directly to the file.

Context Switching:
GOMAXPROCS(1) keeps goroutine scheduling on one OS thread. Allowing multiple OS threads can change the switching cost because scheduling and synchronization may occur across threads.
